Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

ClipSpike is operated by a private individual. Contact: ehrlichjurgen99@gmail.com.

What data we process

ClipSpike does not collect personal data from visitors of this website and does not use cookies or analytics. The tool reads publicly available Twitch chat messages only to count message activity; individual chat messages are not stored or published. Clips contain publicly broadcast stream content of the respective streamer.

TikTok

ClipSpike connects only to its own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API to upload clips. Access tokens are stored as protected environment variables on the hosting service and are never shared. No data of other TikTok users is accessed.

Third parties

Transcripts of clip audio may be sent to AI providers (Groq, Google) to generate titles and hashtags. Hosting is provided by Render.

Your rights

If a clip shows you and you want it removed, write to the address above and it will be deleted.
