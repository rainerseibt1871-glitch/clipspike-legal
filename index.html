ClipSpike

ClipSpike is a small tool that finds the most exciting moments of Twitch livestreams and turns them into short vertical highlight clips with captions.

How it works

The tool watches the public Twitch chat of selected channels. When chat activity spikes, it saves the surrounding moment of the stream, crops it to 9:16 and adds subtitles and a short title. The finished clip is posted to the ClipSpike TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

What it is not

ClipSpike does not collect data from TikTok users, does not offer accounts or logins to visitors, and does not sell anything. It only publishes clips to its own TikTok account.
