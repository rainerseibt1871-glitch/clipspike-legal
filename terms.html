ClipSpike is a personal, single-user automation tool that monitors a Twitch livestream the account owner has chosen, detects highlight-worthy moments, and publishes short video clips to TikTok on behalf of the account owner using the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Scope

These Terms govern the use of the ClipSpike application ("the App") by its owner and operator. The App is not offered as a public product or service to third parties; it operates on a single TikTok account authorized directly by its owner.

No content is posted to any account other than the one that granted authorization, and authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's connected-apps settings.

All video content published by the App is sourced from livestream footage that the account owner has the rights to clip and publish. The account owner is solely responsible for ensuring published content complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
