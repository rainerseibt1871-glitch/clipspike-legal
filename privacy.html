ClipSpike — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

ClipSpike is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by its owner. It is not distributed to or used by third parties. This policy explains what data the App processes and how.

1. Data We Process

  • TikTok account tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the App, used only to publish videos to that one account.
  • Video content: Short video clips generated from the account owner's own livestream footage, processed locally and uploaded directly to TikTok via the Content Posting API.

The App does not collect data about TikTok viewers, followers, or any third party, and does not process data belonging to anyone other than the account owner.

2. How Data Is Used

Tokens and video content are used exclusively to publish content to the single authorized TikTok account. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Access tokens are stored securely on infrastructure controlled by the account owner and are deleted if authorization is revoked. Video files are retained only as long as needed to complete publishing and for basic operational logging.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, except as required to interact with the TikTok API itself in order to publish content.

5. Your Rights

The account owner may revoke the App's access at any time via TikTok's account settings, which immediately stops any further use of the associated tokens.
